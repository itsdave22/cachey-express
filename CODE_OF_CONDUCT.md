# Code of Conduct

Cachey is intended to be a welcoming, professional, and constructive open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- Treat other people with respect.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking individuals.
- Give useful, specific, and good-faith feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, repeated disruption, or deliberate derailing of discussions.
- Sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention.
- Attempts to exploit security reports, community members, or project infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

If you need to report a community conduct issue privately, use the official contact channel listed at https://cachey.eu.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to `SECURITY.md`, not through the conduct process.

## Scope

This policy applies to Cachey-managed project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official community channels.

We want technical disagreement to remain possible without making collaboration hostile. Be clear, be constructive, and treat people fairly.
